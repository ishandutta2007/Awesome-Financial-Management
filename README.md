# Awesome-Financial-Management

## Top Financial Management Ecosystem



**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**  

*Focused on Enterprise Accounting, Financial Planning & Treasury Operations*  

**Last updated: October 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Financial Management**. These tools enable organizations to manage core accounting, financial planning and analysis (FP&A), treasury operations, accounts payable automation, and regulatory reporting—providing the financial visibility and controls required for sound business governance.



**Examples** include ServiceNow Financial Management, Workday Financial Management, Oracle NetSuite, SAP S/4HANA Finance, Sage Intacct, Microsoft Dynamics 365 Finance, Kyriba, BlackLine, Anaplan, and Tipalti (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: This section is expanded with active projects for self-hosted ERP accounting, double-entry bookkeeping, and accounts payable automation—ideal for organizations building sovereign financial infrastructure without per-module or per-user licensing lock-in.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms



- **[ServiceNow Financial Management](https://www.servicenow.com/)**  

  Financial management capabilities on the Now Platform for IT financial management, budgeting, and cost transparency; integrates with Strategic Portfolio Management for investment tracking and CMDB for service costing.



- **[Workday Financial Management](https://www.workday.com/)**  

  Cloud-native financial management suite with core accounting, financial accounting, revenue management, and expense tracking; unified with Workday HCM on a single data model for real-time workforce and financial insights.



- **[Oracle NetSuite](https://www.netsuite.com/)**  

  Cloud ERP with comprehensive financial management including general ledger, accounts receivable/payable, cash management, revenue recognition, and multi-currency consolidation for mid-market and growing enterprises.



- **[SAP S/4HANA Finance](https://www.sap.com/)**  

  Enterprise financial management within SAP's intelligent ERP; provides universal journal for single source of financial truth, advanced financial closing, treasury and risk management, and predictive accounting capabilities.



- **[Sage Intacct](https://www.sageintacct.com/)**  

  Cloud financial management platform for mid-market companies with core accounting, billing, expense management, and financial reporting; strong for professional services and subscription-based businesses.



- **[Microsoft Dynamics 365 Finance](https://dynamics.microsoft.com/en-us/finance/)**  

  Microsoft's cloud financial management solution with general ledger, accounts payable/receivable, budgeting, fixed assets, and financial reporting; integrates with the broader Dynamics 365 and Power Platform ecosystem.



- **[Kyriba](https://www.kyriba.com/)**  

  Treasury management platform for cash visibility, liquidity planning, payments, and risk management; provides real-time bank connectivity and AI-powered cash forecasting for enterprise treasury teams.



- **[BlackLine](https://www.blackline.com/)**  

  Financial close automation and accounting operations platform; streamlines reconciliation, transaction matching, task management, and journal entry workflows for faster, more accurate month-end close.



- **[Anaplan](https://www.anaplan.com/)**  

  Connected planning platform for financial planning and analysis (FP&A), budgeting, forecasting, and scenario modeling; enables collaborative planning across finance, sales, and operations with real-time what-if analysis.



- **[Tipalti](https://tipalti.com/)**  

  AI-powered accounts payable automation with invoice capture, approval workflows, global payments to 200+ countries, and tax compliance (W-9, W-8, VAT); integrates with NetSuite, QuickBooks, and major ERPs.



## Open-Source GitHub Projects



- **[ERPNext](https://github.com/frappe/erpnext)**  

  Leading open-source ERP (GPL-3.0, ~6,500 GitHub stars in Linux Foundation's accounting collection) with comprehensive financial management: double-entry accounting, chart of accounts, journal entries, accounts receivable/payable, bank reconciliation, multi-currency, tax templates, and 25+ financial reports; built on Frappe Framework (Python/Vue) with REST API, role-based permissions, and Docker deployment; the most complete open-source ERP accounting foundation available .



- **[Dolibarr](https://github.com/Dolibarr/dolibarr)**  

  Modular open-source ERP & CRM (GPL-3.0) with double-entry accounting, bank account management, invoices, orders, payments, tax management, and expense reports; supports country-specific tax systems (French, Spanish, Canadian, Indian GST, Saudi ZATCA e-invoicing); available as cloud or on-premise with auto-installers for Windows, Debian, and RPM-based systems; simple to install and customize .



- **[Akaunting](https://github.com/akaunting/akaunting)**  

  Open-source online accounting software for small businesses and freelancers; features invoicing, expense tracking, bank accounts, reconciliation, and financial reports; modular architecture with app store for extensions; ~1,000 GitHub stars in Linux Foundation's collection .



- **[Firefly III](https://github.com/firefly-iii/firefly-iii)**  

  Self-hosted personal finance manager (AGPL-3.0, ~4,900 GitHub stars) with double-entry bookkeeping, budgets, categories, tags, and financial reports; supports bank import via CSV and third-party connectors; strong privacy focus with Docker deployment; best suited for individual and small-team financial tracking rather than enterprise accounting .



- **[GnuCash](https://github.com/Gnucash/gnucash)**  

  Long-established open-source double-entry accounting software (GPL) for personal and small business finance; available as desktop application with features for accounts, transactions, reports, and scheduled transactions; ~1,000 GitHub stars with excellent maintenance score in Linux Foundation's assessment .



- **[LedgerSMB](https://github.com/ledgersmb/LedgerSMB)**  

  Open-source double-entry accounting and ERP system (GPL-2.0) for small to mid-sized businesses; features general ledger, accounts payable/receivable, invoicing, inventory, and financial reporting; web-based with PostgreSQL backend; actively maintained but smaller community than ERPNext .



- **[Frappe Books](https://github.com/frappe/books)**  

  Free desktop accounting software (GPL-3.0) for small businesses and freelancers; simple double-entry bookkeeping with invoicing, payments, and reports; built as a desktop app (Electron) with local SQLite storage; part of the Frappe ecosystem .



- **[dubbl](https://github.com/dubbl-org/dubbl)**  

  Full-featured open-source alternative to Xero and QuickBooks (API-first); covers double-entry bookkeeping, invoicing, bills/POs, banking, expense management, inventory, payroll, projects, CRM, fixed assets, budgets, multi-currency, 25+ financial reports, and full audit trail; includes a **built-in MCP Server** for AI agent integration via OAuth 2.1; Next.js + PostgreSQL stack with Docker self-hosting support .



- **[Ledgic](https://github.com/alihdrndm/ledgic)**  

  Open-source accounts-payable automation platform (Next.js + FastAPI + Supabase); features AI extraction (GPT-4.1-mini vision), three-way matching, tool-using agents, RAG copilot, and an MCP server; enforces segregation of duties and row-level security in the database layer; Docker-local development with fixture mode for zero-cost testing .



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options



- **OCA (Odoo Community Association) modules** — Community-maintained accounting modules for Odoo ERP, including bank payment, POS, and analytic accounting; extend Odoo's native accounting capabilities .

- **hledger** — Plain-text accounting tool (GPL) for developers and power users who want version-controlled, scriptable financial records; excellent for automation and reproducible reporting .

- **Beancount** — Double-entry bookkeeping from plain-text files; popular among technical users for its auditability and integration with scripting workflows.



**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Combine **ERPNext** for a complete ERP accounting foundation with 25+ reports and REST API, **Dolibarr** for modular SMB accounting with country-specific tax support, **dubbl** for a modern Xero/QuickBooks alternative with AI/MCP integration, and **Ledgic** for AP automation with three-way matching and RAG copilot in a self-hosted financial management stack.

## Disclaimer



- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.

- Financial management platforms handling accounting records, payroll, and tax data must comply with applicable regulations (GAAP/IFRS, SOX, local tax laws, GDPR for payroll data) and organizational financial controls; audit trails and segregation of duties are critical.

- Self-hosted open-source solutions vary significantly in enterprise readiness—ERPNext and Dolibarr provide mature ERP accounting, while dubbl and Ledgic offer modern API-first architectures with AI integration; full enterprise financial consolidation, multi-entity reporting, and regulatory filing may still require commercial platforms or significant customization.

- Verify license terms for commercial use; ERPNext uses GPL-3.0, Dolibarr uses GPL-3.0, Firefly III uses AGPL-3.0, dubbl is open-source (check repository), and Ledgic is open-source .
